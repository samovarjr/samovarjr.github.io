Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Hermes is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, Hermes accesses the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive through Google APIs to read, summarize, label and draft messages and events.

Use and sharing

Data is used solely to provide assistant features to the owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties, except that content may be processed by the AI model providers the assistant runs on, only to produce responses for the owner.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored on the assistant's private server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
